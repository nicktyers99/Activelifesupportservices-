Pathways

A clear, supported journey from your first enquiry through to ongoing, person-centred support.

How support with us works

Every participant's path looks different — here's the general journey we take together.

1. Get in touch

Reach out via phone, email, or our contact form to tell us about your goals and support needs.

2. Meet the team

We'll arrange a time to meet, understand your NDIS plan, and talk through the right supports for you.

3. Build your plan

Together we'll design a support plan tailored to your goals, routines, and preferences.

4. Ongoing support

We check in regularly and adjust your supports as your goals and circumstances change.

Get in touch and we'll help you take the first step.
